MUNICIPAL ANALYTICS PROJECT

Does cultural expenditure influence citizen satisfaction?

This project investigates how municipal spending relates to citizen perceptions across Flemish municipalities, using data from the Flemish Gemeente-Stadsmonitor. The interactive dashboard integrates financial indicators with citizen survey data, covering domains such as community life, social cohesion, mobility, and local livability. The visualization aims to support comparative analysis and benchmarking between municipalities. In particular, it seeks to uncover whether municipalities with similar characteristics exhibit differing relationships between public investment and citizen satisfaction.

PROJECT DESIGN

Methodology

Dataset

Vlaamse Gemeente-Stadsmonitor

Variables

Cultural expenditure and satisfaction indicators

Coverage

Flemish municipalities

Approach

Interactive visual analytics using Tableau

Target Users

The target users are municipal council members and local and Flemish policymakers involved in budget discussions and policy evaluation. Municipal councils are responsible for allocating local budgets while addressing citizen concerns and perceptions. Although financial reports and citizen surveys are publicly available, these data sources are often explored separately. This visualization bridges that gap by combining expenditure and perception data into a single comparative and exploratory interface.

01

Regression Analysis

Investigating the relationship between cultural expenditure and municipal satisfaction.

Key Takeaway

The regression dashboard explores whether municipalities with higher cultural expenditure tend to achieve stronger citizen satisfaction outcomes.

02

Municipality Comparison

Comparing municipalities and exploring local differences in performance.

Key Takeaway

Interactive comparison enables identification of municipalities that outperform or underperform relative to their expenditure levels.

INSIGHTS

Key Findings

01

Positive relationship observed

Municipalities with higher expenditure generally demonstrate stronger satisfaction outcomes.

02

Outliers exist

Some municipalities perform better than expected despite relatively modest expenditure.

03

Context matters

Expenditure alone cannot fully explain differences in satisfaction levels.

Limitations

This analysis identifies relationships but does not establish causality. Additional socio-economic, demographic and policy factors may influence satisfaction outcomes.
